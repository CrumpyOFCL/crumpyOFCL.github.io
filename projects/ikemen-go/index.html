IkemanGoAss

Designed by Tyler Crump

Designed by Tyler Crump. A solo design project in Ikemen GO, the open-source fighting game engine it's built on. Character and moveset design for The Sword Saint and Unknown, combat systems for their shared moveset, and the Broken Bridge stage.

Checkpoint 4What I did

  • Character and moveset design for two fighters, The Sword Saint and Unknown. Unknown is a mirror fighter who shares the same moveset.
  • Combat systems design: 4 normals, a two-projectile lightning special, a meter-gated super, and a back dash, plus block and a timed perfect block, and a stamina system.
  • Stage design: Broken Bridge, a 6-layer storm bridge stage with animated lightning and rain.
