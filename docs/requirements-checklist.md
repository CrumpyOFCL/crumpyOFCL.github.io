# Requirements checklist

Read against the three junior briefs supplied with this project. Wording here is the portfolio's, so the public site does not repeat the ads.

Status:

- **Demonstrated** — a reviewer can open the artefact.
- **Described** — Tyler's account, or a write-up of work a reviewer cannot open.
- **Pending** — a slot is on the site; the evidence is not.
- **Gap** — not shown, and not claimed.

Nothing here upgrades a description into a demonstration.
