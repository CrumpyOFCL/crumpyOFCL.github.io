01 / THE GOAL

What I set out to build.

Bring movement, time-based mechanics and player progression together into a readable platforming experience.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution.

  • Programmed moving-platform passenger behaviour and dash/jump/audio systems.
  • Worked on the skill tree, save/load, HUD and time-beam systems.
  • Fixed WebGL audio behaviour and worked with URP particles.
  • Led sprint planning, workload coordination and retrospectives; produced the final production report for sprints 2–4.

This was a team project. The responsibilities above describe my contributions, not sole ownership of the complete project.
