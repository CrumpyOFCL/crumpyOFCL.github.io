About

Hi, I'm Tyler.

I'm in the final year of a Bachelor of Game Design and Development at Macquarie University, finishing in 2026. The work I enjoy most is making a rule hold up once players start leaning on it: what happens when two eras overlap on the spot you're standing, or which door stays open after a reload.

On team projects I've usually been the one holding the plan: sprint planning, retrospectives, client emails and playtest sessions. On my own I've designed a two-fighter combat kit in Ikemen GO and built a group trip planner whose first real test was a group trip to Japan.

Open to junior game design, gameplay and tools roles.
